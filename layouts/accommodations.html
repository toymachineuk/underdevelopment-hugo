CAPITAL

  • Old Tbilisi Historic streets, balconies and sulphur baths
  • Vake Leafy streets, cafés and contemporary city life
  • Avlabari Hillside neighbourhood overlooking the old city
Getting there
Tbilisi International Airport → City
  • 🚇 Metro: fast city connections
  • 🚕 Taxi / Bolt: widely available
  • 🚌 Bus: extensive city network
Georgia's capital, blending medieval streets, modern architecture, sulphur baths, cafés, markets and a lively cultural scene.

Adjara

Towns & Villages
  • Batumi Seaside city with beaches, towers and nightlife
  • Kobuleti Relaxed Black Sea resort with a long pebble beach
  • Khulo Mountain town surrounded by dramatic landscapes
Getting there
From Tbilisi → Batumi
  • 🚆 Train: regular Tbilisi–Batumi service
  • 🚗 Car: roughly 5–6 hours
  • ✈️ Flight: Batumi International Airport
Georgia's subtropical Black Sea region, combining beaches and seaside cities with lush mountains, waterfalls and traditional villages.

  • Shekvetili Quiet coastal resort with pine forests and beaches
Getting there
From Tbilisi → Ozurgeti

  • 🚌 Marshrutka / bus: regular services

  • Tskaltubo Former spa town surrounded by forests and caves
  • Chiatura Dramatic mining town crossed by steep valleys
Getting there
From Tbilisi → Kutaisi
  • 🚆 Train: regular connections
  • 🚗 Car: roughly 3.5–4 hours
  • 🚌 Marshrutka / bus: regular services

  • Stepantsminda Mountain town beneath Mount Kazbek

CAUCASUS

  • Zugdidi Regional centre and gateway to Svaneti
  • Mestia Mountain town surrounded by Svan towers and peaks
  • Ushguli High mountain community beneath the Greater Caucasus
Getting there
From Tbilisi → Mestia
  • ✈️ Flight: Tbilisi → Mestia, seasonal availability
