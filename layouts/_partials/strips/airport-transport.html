Tbilisi Transport

Getting to the City Center

Private Airport Transfer If you are traveling with children, carrying lots of luggage, or arriving late at night, a pre-booked private transfer can make your arrival much easier. Your driver will arrange to meet you at the airport, usually inside the arrivals area with a name sign, and take you directly to your accommodation. Confirm the meeting instructions with your provider before departure.
Bus 337 (Budget Route) If you're feeling adventurous and traveling light, the 337 Public Bus departs from right outside the arrivals terminal. Details: Runs into central Tbilisi (ending at Station Square). You can simply tap your contactless Visa/Mastercard directly on the bus validator. Note: Runs regularly during daytime hours until late evening (~11:00 PM).
Taxi Be cautious if someone approaches you inside the arrivals terminal offering an unofficial taxi or claiming to be your app-booked driver. Do not accept unsolicited rides. Some drivers may quote inflated fares — potentially £70 / $90 or more for a journey that could cost substantially less through a ride-hailing app. Prices vary, so always check the fare before traveling. Instead, book your ride directly through Bolt, confirm your driver's details in the app, or arrange a private transfer in advance through a reputable provider.
