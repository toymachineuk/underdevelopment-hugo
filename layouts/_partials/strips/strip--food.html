Georgian cuisine

A taste of Georgia

Khachapuri Khachapuri is a traditional Georgian bread filled with melted cheese.
Kharcho Kharcho is a traditional Georgian soup made with beef, rice, walnuts, and aromatic spices.
Khinkali Khinkali are juicy Georgian dumplings packed with spiced meat, herbs, and rich broth.
Satsivi Satsivi is tender chicken in a rich, creamy walnut sauce, spiced with Georgian herbs.
Ojakhuri Ojakhuri is a hearty Georgian dish of juicy pork, crispy potatoes, onions, and herbs.
Lobio Lobio is a comforting Georgian bean stew packed with herbs, spices, and rich earthy flavour.
