Georgian wine

Discover Georgian Wine

From bold reds and crisp whites to traditional amber wines, discover the flavours of one of the world's oldest winemaking cultures.

03

Georgia's winemaking tradition stretches back thousands of years. Explore its indigenous grapes, distinctive wines, and ancient qvevri fermentation methods.

Saperavi A bold, dry red wine known for its deep colour, rich dark fruit flavours, and firm tannins.
Mukuzani A structured dry red made from Saperavi grapes, with dark berry flavours and elegant oak notes.
Kindzmarauli A naturally semi-sweet red wine with soft tannins and rich cherry and blackberry flavours.
Tsinandali A refreshing dry white wine with bright acidity, delicate floral aromas, and citrus notes.
Rkatsiteli Qvevri A traditional amber wine fermented in clay qvevri, with dried fruit, herbal notes, and gentle tannins.
Kisi Qvevri An aromatic amber wine with ripe stone fruit, nutty notes, and a rich, textured finish.
