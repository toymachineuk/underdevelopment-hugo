Animals

You can meet them in Georgia.

A quick guide to some of Georgia's rare and remarkable animals.

Brown Bear Region: Guria, Adjara and the Caucasus mountains.
Caucasian Leopard One of Georgia's rarest mammals, found in remote mountain landscapes.
Caucasian Chamois Region: Greater Caucasus. A sure-footed mountain animal found on steep alpine slopes.
Eastern Imperial Eagle A powerful bird of prey seen across Georgia during migration and in open landscapes.
Eurasian Otter Found around clean rivers, lakes and wetlands across Georgia.
Caucasian Red Deer A large native deer found in Georgia's forests and protected areas.
Bearded Vulture A spectacular mountain bird that soars above the high Caucasus.
