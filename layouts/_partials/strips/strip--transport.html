Getting around

A few ways to get around.

Metro Map Lines & stations ↗
How to pay? Use a Metromoney card or contactless payment for public transport in Tbilisi.
Metro Tbilisi's metro is one of the quickest and easiest ways to get around the city.
Bus City buses connect Tbilisi's neighbourhoods and are useful for reaching places beyond the metro.
Minibus Marshrutkas are a common way to travel between towns and cities, especially where trains don't reach.
Taxi Taxis are convenient for short trips, late-night journeys, and places that are harder to reach by public transport.
