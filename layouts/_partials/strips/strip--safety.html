Safety Tips

Practical advice to help you travel confidently around Tbilisi and the rest of Georgia.

Is Georgia safe? Georgia welcomes many travellers. Stay aware of your surroundings and take normal precautions in busy areas.
Emergency number Dial 112 for police, ambulance or fire services throughout Georgia.
Crossing the road Traffic can be busy. Use marked crossings and check carefully before stepping onto the road.
Money & scams Check taxi fares in an app, confirm prices in advance and keep valuables secure in crowded places.
British Embassy 51 Krtsanisi Street, Tbilisi. Check official contact details and opening hours before visiting.
Good to know Keep a passport copy, carry some lari and check official travel advice before visiting border areas.
