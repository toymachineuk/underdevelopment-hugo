Is Georgia safe? Georgia is a popular destination for travellers. As in any country, stay aware of your surroundings and take normal precautions, especially in crowded areas.
Emergency number Call 112 for police, an ambulance or the fire service. The number operates throughout Georgia. Save it on your phone before your trip.
Crossing the road Traffic can be busy, and driving styles vary. Use pedestrian crossings where available and check carefully in both directions before crossing.
Money & scams Check taxi fares through a ride-hailing app, confirm prices before ordering, and keep an eye on your belongings in busy tourist areas.
British Embassy British Embassy Tbilisi 51 Krtsanisi Street, Tbilisi 0114 Open Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm
Good to know Keep a copy of your passport, carry some Georgian lari for small purchases, and check current travel advice before visiting border areas.
