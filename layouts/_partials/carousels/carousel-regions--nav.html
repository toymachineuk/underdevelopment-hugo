A subtropical coastline of seaside towns, lush mountains and traditional villages.

Places to explore

Batumi

Coastal city and nightlife.

Kobuleti

Long pebble beach.

Khulo

Mountain landscapes.

Getting there
By train

Tbilisi to Batumi.

By road

Around 5–6 hours from Tbilisi.

By air

Batumi International Airport.

Imereti

Western Georgia · Rioni Valley

A region of ancient monasteries, limestone caves, green valleys and distinctive Georgian cuisine.

Places to explore

Kutaisi

Historic city with lively markets and old architecture.

Tskaltubo

Former spa town known for its Soviet-era sanatoriums.

Prometheus Cave

Impressive underground chambers and limestone formations.

Getting there
By train

Connections from Tbilisi to Kutaisi.

By road

Around 3–4 hours from Tbilisi to Kutaisi.

By air

David the Builder Kutaisi International Airport.

Kakheti

Eastern Georgia · Alazani Valley

Ancient wine culture, vineyard landscapes, historic monasteries and mountain views.

Getting there
By car

Around 2–3 hours from Tbilisi to Telavi.

Minibus

Regular departures from Tbilisi to Telavi and Sighnaghi.

Main towns

Telavi

Historic heart of Kakheti.

Sighnaghi

Hilltop town with valley views.

Kvareli

Vineyards and wine cellars.

Things to do
  • Wine tasting
  • Traditional Georgian food
  • Monasteries and heritage sites
  • Hiking and nature
Known for
  • Traditional qvevri winemaking
  • Vineyard landscapes
  • Historic monasteries
  • Georgian hospitality

Kvemo Kartli

Southern Georgia · South of Tbilisi

A crossroads of ancient civilizations, medieval landmarks and striking landscapes just south of Tbilisi.

Places to explore

Dmanisi

Archaeological site of 1.8-million-year-old human remains.

Bolnisi

Historic town known for its 5th-century basilica.

Dashbashi Canyon

Dramatic green canyon with waterfalls and the Diamond Bridge.

Getting there
By road

Most destinations are around 1–2 hours from Tbilisi.

By minibus

Connections from Tbilisi to the main towns; local sights may require a car or taxi.

Mtskheta-Mtianeti

Eastern Georgia · Greater Caucasus

From Georgia’s ancient capital to dramatic mountain valleys, medieval fortresses and alpine landscapes.

Places to explore

Mtskheta

Ancient capital with UNESCO-listed churches and monasteries.

Stepantsminda

Mountain town beneath Mount Kazbek and Gergeti Trinity Church.

Gudauri

Mountain resort known for skiing and spectacular views.

Getting there
By minibus

Frequent departures from Tbilisi to Mtskheta; services to Stepantsminda from Didube.

By road

Around 30 minutes to Mtskheta and 3 hours to Stepantsminda.

Racha-Lechkhumi & Kvemo Svaneti

Northwestern Georgia · Caucasus Mountains

A peaceful mountain region of deep valleys, alpine landscapes, traditional villages and distinctive wines.

Places to explore

Ambrolauri

Heart of Racha, known for Khvanchkara wine.

Oni

Mountain town with historic architecture and Jewish heritage.

Tsageri

Gateway to Lechkhumi’s green valleys and mountain villages.

Getting there
By road

Around 4–5 hours from Tbilisi to Ambrolauri.

By minibus

Services to Ambrolauri and other regional towns are limited.

Samegrelo-Zemo Svaneti

Western Georgia · Black Sea & Greater Caucasus

From subtropical wetlands and ancient fortresses to remote mountain villages beneath the Caucasus peaks.

Places to explore

Mestia

Mountain town known for medieval Svan towers and alpine scenery.

Ushguli

High-altitude villages with ancient towers and views of Shkhara.

Martvili Canyon

Lush gorge with turquoise water, waterfalls and boat trips.

Getting there
By road

Around 8–10 hours from Tbilisi to Mestia.

By air

Flights between Natakhtari and Mestia, subject to schedule and weather.

Samtskhe-Javakheti

Southern Georgia · Lesser Caucasus

A land of medieval cave cities, ancient fortresses, volcanic plateaus and peaceful high-altitude lakes.

Places to explore

Vardzia

Remarkable cave monastery carved into a mountainside.

Akhaltsikhe

Historic town dominated by the restored Rabati Fortress.

Paravani Lake

Vast high-altitude lake surrounded by open volcanic landscapes.

Getting there
By road

Around 3–4 hours from Tbilisi to Akhaltsikhe.

By minibus

Services from Tbilisi to Akhaltsikhe; onward travel may require a car or taxi.

Shida Kartli

Central Georgia · Mtkvari Valley

A historic heartland of Georgia, with ancient cave towns, medieval monuments and fertile river valleys.

Places to explore

Gori

Historic town and birthplace of Joseph Stalin.

Uplistsikhe

Ancient rock-hewn town with temples and cave dwellings.

Kaspi

Quiet town near historic churches and the Samtavisi Cathedral.

Getting there
By train

Connections from Tbilisi to Gori.

By road

Around 1–1.5 hours from Tbilisi to Gori.
