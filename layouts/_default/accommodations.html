HOW WE SELECT ACCOMMODATION
01 / FIRST IMPRESSIONS

The Façade Matters

The exterior of a building can be part of the experience, especially among the historic streets and distinctive architecture of Tbilisi's Old Town.

But appearances only tell part of the story. Some of the most welcoming hotels and apartments may look unremarkable outside, yet surprise you with beautiful interiors.

02 / WHAT REALLY MATTERS

Beyond Appearances

Our selection considers more than photographs and first impressions. We pay attention to location, comfort and the atmosphere of a place.

Is it somewhere you can enjoy a quiet evening or meet fellow travellers? Finding the right accommodation is about balancing different priorities.

We're simply here to encourage you to explore that balance for yourself. Here is our selection.
