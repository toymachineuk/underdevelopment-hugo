Safety & Entry Requirements

Travel Insurance

Travel health and accident insurance is mandatory for tourists entering Georgia. Border control officers may ask you to provide proof of valid coverage for the duration of your stay.

Keep a digital copy on your phone or carry a printed policy in English or Georgian. Make sure your insurance covers medical emergencies and accidents throughout your visit.

The Smart Strategy

Money & Card Payments

You’ll find plenty of currency exchange booths at Tbilisi Airport, but there’s no need to exchange large sums of cash on arrival. Card payments are widely accepted across Tbilisi, including in shops, restaurants and many transport services.

Skip large airport exchanges. Airport exchange counters may offer less favourable rates. If you need cash right away, consider withdrawing a small amount of Georgian lari (GEL) from an ATM operated by a reputable bank, such as Bank of Georgia or TBC Bank.

Option A · Recommended

Bolt App

Bolt is the easiest option for most visitors — similar to Uber. Request a ride through the app and see the fare before confirming, without negotiating with drivers at the airport.

Typical price: Around 23 GEL (approximately £8 / $10 USD) to central Tbilisi, including areas around Freedom Square and Rustaveli. Fares vary with demand and your destination.

Download the Bolt app before your trip, add your debit or credit card, and follow the app's instructions to meet your driver at the designated pickup point outside the terminal.
