Entry Requirements

Travel Insurance

Travel health and accident insurance is mandatory for tourists entering Georgia. Border control officers may ask you to provide proof of valid coverage for the duration of your stay.

Keep a digital copy on your phone or carry a printed policy in English or Georgian. Make sure your insurance covers medical emergencies and accidents throughout your visit.

Minimum coverage 30,000 GEL

Medical expenses and accidents must be covered for your entire stay, including arrival and departure days.

Estimated cost 17–25 GEL

An indicative price for basic 10-day coverage. Actual prices depend on your age, provider and policy.

Where to buy Online before arrival

Compare Georgian providers such as Unison, ARDI and IRAO, or choose an international policy that meets Georgia's requirements.

02
Everyday essentials

Money in Georgia

The Georgian lari (GEL) is the local currency. Card payments are widely accepted in Tbilisi, but keeping a little cash handy is useful for markets, smaller shops and travel beyond the city.

Save on exchange fees

You don't need to exchange large amounts of cash at the airport. If you need Georgian lari on arrival, consider withdrawing a small amount from a reputable bank ATM.

When paying or withdrawing cash, choosing GEL instead of your home currency can help avoid additional conversion markups.

01 / EVERYDAY PAYMENTS

Contactless cards

Visa and Mastercard are widely accepted across Tbilisi. Contactless cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay are convenient for everyday purchases wherever supported.

02 / CURRENCY EXCHANGE

Check your exchange fees

Banks, ATMs and currency-exchange offices may offer different rates and fees. Compare your options before converting money or withdrawing cash.

03
Airport transport

Getting to Central Tbilisi

From affordable airport buses to door-to-door transfers, there are several ways to reach central Tbilisi. Choose the option that suits your arrival time, luggage and budget.

Avoid unofficial taxis

Be cautious of unsolicited taxi offers inside the arrivals terminal. Some drivers may charge considerably more than app-booked rides.

For a more predictable journey, book through a ride-hailing app such as Bolt or arrange a transfer in advance. Always confirm your driver's details and fare before getting into the car.

01 / DOOR-TO-DOOR

Taxi & Ride-Hailing

A taxi is one of the easiest ways to reach your accommodation, especially if you're travelling with luggage. Ride-hailing apps such as Bolt let you check the estimated fare and driver details before pickup.

Avoid accepting unsolicited rides inside the terminal. Follow your app's pickup instructions and confirm the vehicle before getting in.

02 / BUDGET-FRIENDLY

Airport Bus 337

Public bus 337 connects Tbilisi International Airport with the city, including central stops and Station Square. It's an affordable option if you're travelling light.

The bus stop is outside the arrivals terminal. Contactless bank cards can be used where supported. Check the latest timetable and payment options before travelling, particularly for late-night arrivals.

03 / PRE-ARRANGED

Private Airport Transfer

A pre-booked transfer is a convenient option for families, groups, travellers with extra luggage or anyone arriving late at night.

Your driver will meet you at an agreed location and take you directly to your accommodation. Confirm the price, pickup point and meeting instructions with your provider before arrival.

Internet & Wi-Fi

Stay Connected in Georgia

Having internet access when you land makes it easier to book a ride, find your accommodation and navigate Tbilisi. From free airport Wi-Fi to eSIMs and local mobile plans, getting connected is straightforward.

Free airport Wi-Fi

Tbilisi International Airport provides free Wi-Fi, so you can get online before purchasing a local mobile plan.

Look for the airport's official network in your Wi-Fi settings. Check the airport website for current connection instructions and any password requirements.
