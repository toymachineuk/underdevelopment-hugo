Entry Requirements

Travel Insurance

Travel health and accident insurance is mandatory for tourists entering Georgia. Border control officers may ask you to provide proof of valid coverage for the duration of your stay.

Keep a digital copy on your phone or carry a printed policy in English or Georgian. Make sure your insurance covers medical emergencies and accidents throughout your visit.

The Smart Strategy

Money & Card Payments

You’ll find plenty of currency exchange booths at Tbilisi Airport, but there’s no need to exchange large sums of cash on arrival. Card payments are widely accepted across Tbilisi, including in shops, restaurants and many transport services.

If you need cash right away, consider withdrawing a small amount of Georgian lari (GEL) from an ATM operated by a reputable bank, such as Bank of Georgia or TBC Bank.

01 / DOOR-TO-DOOR

Taxi & Ride-Hailing

Be cautious if someone approaches you inside the arrivals terminal offering an unofficial taxi or claiming to be your app-booked driver. Do not accept unsolicited rides. Some drivers may quote inflated fares — potentially £70 / $90 or more for a journey that could cost substantially less through a ride-hailing app. Prices vary, so always check the fare before traveling. Instead, book your ride directly through Bolt, confirm your driver's details in the app, or arrange a private transfer in advance through a reputable provider.

02 / BUDGET-FRIENDLY

Airport Bus

Bus 337 (Budget Route) If you're feeling adventurous and traveling light, the 337 Public Bus departs from right outside the arrivals terminal. Details: Runs into central Tbilisi (ending at Station Square). You can simply tap your contactless Visa/Mastercard directly on the bus validator. Note: Runs regularly during daytime hours until late evening (~11:00 PM).

03 / PRE-ARRANGED

Private Transfer

If you are traveling with children, carrying lots of luggage, or arriving late at night, a pre-booked private transfer can make your arrival much easier. Your driver will arrange to meet you at the airport, usually inside the arrivals area with a name sign, and take you directly to your accommodation. Confirm the meeting instructions with your provider before departure.
