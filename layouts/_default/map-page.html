Winter in Georgia

Skiing in Georgia

From high-altitude mountain resorts to snowy villages, Georgia offers fantastic winter escapes in the Caucasus. Discover where to ski, when to visit and what to expect on the slopes.

Plan Your Mountain Escape

Georgia has several ski destinations, each with its own character. Gudauri is known for its wide slopes and freeride opportunities, Bakuriani is popular with families and beginners, while Mestia offers spectacular mountain scenery and access to the slopes of Tetnuldi and Hatsvali. Choose your resort based on your skiing experience, travel plans and the kind of mountain adventure you want.

01 / GUDAURI

Gudauri — High-Altitude Skiing

Located along the Georgian Military Highway, Gudauri is Georgia's best-known ski resort. It offers extensive slopes for different skill levels, modern lifts, spectacular mountain views and excellent freeride terrain for experienced skiers.

02 / BAKURIANI

Bakuriani — Perfect for Families

Surrounded by pine forests in the Lesser Caucasus, Bakuriani is a favourite for families, beginners and relaxed winter holidays. Its ski areas include Didveli, Kokhta and Mitarbi, with slopes for different experience levels.

03 / SVANETI

Mestia — Skiing in the Caucasus

For a more dramatic mountain setting, head to Svaneti. The Tetnuldi and Hatsvali ski areas offer beautiful views of the Caucasus, varied terrain and a chance to combine skiing with the region's unique medieval villages and traditions.

Natural Wellness in Georgia

Health & Wellness in Georgia

Discover Georgia's famous mineral springs, thermal baths and historic health resorts. From traditional mud treatments to mountain spa retreats, explore destinations where nature and wellness come together.

Find Your Wellness Escape

Georgia has a long tradition of balneotherapy and natural wellness. Tskaltubo is famous for its warm mineral springs, Sairme for its mineral and thermal waters, while Akhtala is known for its therapeutic mud. Borjomi combines mineral-water heritage with modern spa facilities, and Nunisi offers a peaceful forest retreat. Choose your destination for relaxation, nature or professionally supervised wellness treatments.

01 / TSKALTUBO

Tskaltubo — Mineral Water & Wellness

Famous for its naturally warm mineral waters (33–35°C), Tskaltubo has a long tradition of balneotherapy. Its spa programmes are designed to support a range of chronic health conditions.

TRADITIONAL TREATMENT AREAS

  • Joint and musculoskeletal conditions
  • Back pain and sciatica
  • Certain neurological conditions
  • Chronic skin conditions, including psoriasis

GETTING THERE

By car Approximately 4 hours from Tbilisi.
By minibus Direct minibuses from Didube Bus Station, or travel via Kutaisi and continue by local minibus or taxi.

SANATORIUM OR PRIVATE HOTEL?

Both options are possible. You can stay at a sanatorium and receive treatment there, or book your own hotel and arrange treatment separately at a local medical or wellness facility. If you choose a private hotel, check whether the treatment facility accepts external guests and whether appointments need to be arranged in advance.

02 / SAIRME

Sairme — Mineral Waters & Mountain Wellness

Sairme is famous for its different mineral-water springs and their traditional use in digestive and metabolic health. Sairme is particularly associated with kidney and urinary health, gallbladder conditions, digestive disorders and metabolic health. Its thermal baths are also used in musculoskeletal and neurological wellness programmes.

TRADITIONAL TREATMENT AREAS

  • Chronic kidney and urinary tract conditions
  • Gallbladder and biliary tract disorders
  • Gastrointestinal conditions, including gastritis and colitis
  • Metabolic disorders, including diabetes-related conditions
  • Musculoskeletal and joint conditions

GETTING THERE

By car Around 4–4.5 hours from Tbilisi to Sairme, via Kutaisi and Baghdati.
By minibus Take a minibus from Tbilisi to Kutaisi, then continue to Baghdati. A taxi is needed for the final 20 km to Sairme.

03 / BORJOMI

Borjomi — Mineral Water & Forest Retreats

Borjomi is particularly famous for its carbonated, sodium-bicarbonate mineral water, traditionally used in drinking-water therapy. Its main wellness focus is the digestive system, liver, gallbladder and metabolism.

TRADITIONAL TREATMENT AREAS

  • Chronic gastritis and stomach disorders
  • Chronic colitis and intestinal disorders
  • Gallbladder and bile duct conditions
  • Chronic liver and pancreatic conditions
  • Metabolic disorders, including certain forms of diabetes

GETTING THERE

By car Around 2.5 hours from Tbilisi to Borjomi.
By minibus Regular minibuses depart from Didube Bus Station to Borjomi, taking approximately 2.5–3 hours.

04 / AKHTALA

Akhtala — Georgia's Famous Mud Resort

Akhtala's main wellness resource is therapeutic mud rather than mineral-water bathing or drinking. Its traditional treatment programmes focus on joint disorders, peripheral nervous system conditions, certain skin conditions and gynaecological conditions.

TRADITIONAL TREATMENT AREAS

  • Osteoarthritis and chronic joint disorders
  • Chronic back pain, sciatica and radiculitis
  • Psoriasis, eczema and neurodermatitis
  • Chronic gynaecological inflammatory conditions
  • Muscle, tendon and soft-tissue disorders

GETTING THERE

By car Around 1.5–2 hours from Tbilisi to Akhtala, near Gurjaani in Kakheti.
By minibus Take a minibus from Tbilisi to Gurjaani. From there, arrange a local taxi to Akhtala.

05 / NUNISI

Nunisi — Mineral Baths in the Forest

Nunisi is particularly famous for its naturally warm, mildly sulphurous mineral water, traditionally used in dermatological balneotherapy. Unlike Borjomi and Sairme, which are strongly associated with drinking mineral water, Nunisi is best known for its mineral baths and treatments for chronic skin conditions. Its forest environment is another important attraction.

TRADITIONAL TREATMENT AREAS

  • Psoriasis
  • Atopic dermatitis (eczema)
  • Seborrheic dermatitis
  • Lichen planus
  • Chronic urticaria (hives)

GETTING THERE

By car Around 3–3.5 hours from Tbilisi to Nunisi, near Kharagauli.
By train Take a train from Tbilisi towards Moliti Railway Station. The resort is approximately 6 km away and can arrange a minibus transfer from the station.
