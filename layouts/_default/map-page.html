Winter in Georgia

Skiing in Georgia

From high-altitude mountain resorts to snowy villages, Georgia offers fantastic winter escapes in the Caucasus. Discover where to ski, when to visit and what to expect on the slopes.

Plan Your Mountain Escape

Georgia has several ski destinations, each with its own character. Gudauri is known for its wide slopes and freeride opportunities, Bakuriani is popular with families and beginners, while Mestia offers spectacular mountain scenery and access to the slopes of Tetnuldi and Hatsvali. Choose your resort based on your skiing experience, travel plans and the kind of mountain adventure you want.

01 / GUDauri

Gudauri — High-Altitude Skiing

Located along the Georgian Military Highway, Gudauri is Georgia's best-known ski resort. It offers extensive slopes for different skill levels, modern lifts, spectacular mountain views and excellent freeride terrain for experienced skiers.

02 / BAKURIANI

Bakuriani — Perfect for Families

Surrounded by pine forests in the Lesser Caucasus, Bakuriani is a favourite for families, beginners and relaxed winter holidays. Its ski areas include Didveli, Kokhta and Mitarbi, with slopes for different experience levels.

03 / SVANETI

Mestia — Skiing in the Caucasus

For a more dramatic mountain setting, head to Svaneti. The Tetnuldi and Hatsvali ski areas offer beautiful views of the Caucasus, varied terrain and a chance to combine skiing with the region's unique medieval villages and traditions.
