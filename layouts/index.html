Money in Georgia

Save on Foreign Conversion Fees

Standard bank cards will charge high exchange markup fees. We strongly recommend using a multi-currency card to get live interbank exchange rates with zero hassle. Consider a Revolut card to exchange currencies in the app and pay in Georgian lari (GEL). Fees and exchange limits depend on your plan and the currency exchange time.
